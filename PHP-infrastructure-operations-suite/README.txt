Infrastructure Operations Suite

Idea:

Four small public examples of business operations systems built with PHP, JavaScript, jQuery, and action-based AJAX.

Included systems:

- Configuration Analyzer
	- Turns device configuration text into understandable network access rules.
- Asset Reconciliation
	- Compares the equipment expected at a site with physical scans.
- Asset Batch Manager
	- Groups validated equipment for shipments, installations, transfers, or other work.
- Operations Toolkit
	- Shares request handling, dialogs, searchable controls, styling, and reports.

How a business could use the suite:

A company could adapt these pieces to manage equipment across offices, warehouses, labs, retail sites, or field locations. The projects show how configuration data, inventory records, physical scanning, controlled batches, and document exports can work together.
