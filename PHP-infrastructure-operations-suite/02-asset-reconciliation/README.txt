Multi-Site Asset Audit and Reconciliation System

Idea:

This system compares the equipment a business expects at a site with the equipment an operator physically scans there. It keeps the original inventory snapshot so later changes do not rewrite the audit history.

How a business could use it:

- Audit equipment in offices, warehouses, labs, or retail locations.
- Scan asset IDs or serial numbers individually or in bulk.
- Identify missing, misplaced, unknown, or pending-review equipment.
- Keep completed audits and repeat an audit against current inventory.
- Import a completed asset batch into an audit.
- Print the result or download it as CSV.

The public demo uses synthetic assets and temporary session storage.
